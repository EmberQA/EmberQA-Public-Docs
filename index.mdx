## What is EmberQA?

EmberQA is the AI-driven QA, Training, and Coaching platform built to turn every call/contact center agent into a top performer. We automate the full QA cycle, surfacing insights, improving agents, and closing performance gaps without manual effort.

Score every customer interaction on autopilot, get personalized coaching insights, train agents to your exact specifications, and catch compliance risks before they become real issues.
