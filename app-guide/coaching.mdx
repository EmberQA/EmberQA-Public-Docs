The Coaching page shows agents who consistently fail specific metrics, helping you identify where targeted coaching would be most effective.

For agents, this page appears as **"Improvement"** and shows only their own data.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| **Team** | Filter by agent tag (managers only) |
| **Days Back** | 1 day, 7 days, or 30 days |
| **Minimum Fail Percentage** | 20% to 80% threshold |
| **Content Type** | Calls or Documents |

## What You See

### Metrics Summary Chart

A chart showing which metrics are failed most often across all agents (managers only).

### Agent List

Each agent with coaching opportunities shows:
- **Agent name** with their average score
- **Number of metrics** needing improvement
- **Table of metrics** with:
  - Total calls/documents
  - Failed count
  - Fail rate percentage
  - **Coaching Insights** button

## Coaching Insights

Click the **Coaching Insights** button on any metric to open an AI-powered analysis.

The AI scans all calls/documents where the agent failed that specific metric and generates:

- **Specific guidance** on how to improve on that metric
- **Concrete examples** from the agent's actual calls showing what to do differently
- **Links to each failed call/document** so you can review them in full detail

This gives managers targeted talking points for 1:1 coaching sessions, and helps agents understand exactly what they need to change.
