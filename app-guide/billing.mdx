Use the **Billing** tab in Account Settings to review credit usage and billing information.

## Check Credit Usage

Start with **Credit Usage** for a summary of current consumption.

Open **Credit Breakdown** when you need to understand which services are consuming credits. Service tracking can include call scoring, document analysis, workflows, and other metered processing depending on your organization's enabled features.

## Manage Billing Details

Use the remaining billing sections to review the current plan, saved payment methods, and billing history.
