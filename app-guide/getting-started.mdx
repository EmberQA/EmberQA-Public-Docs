## Logging In

1. Go to [app.emberqa.com](https://app.emberqa.com)
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**

<Tip>
Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen to receive a reset link.
</Tip>

## Navigation

The sidebar on the left organizes all features into sections:

| Section | Pages |
|---------|-------|
| **Operations** | Dashboard, Daily Reports, Workflows |
| **Performance** | Analytics, Coaching, Training |
| **Standards** | Rubrics, Red Flags, Alerts |
| **Ingestion** | Upload |
| **Settings** | Users, Agent Management, Metadata |
