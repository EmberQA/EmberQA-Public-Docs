The Training page lets managers build structured training programs and assign them to agents. Agents use the same page to complete their assigned training.

## Choose the Right View

Managers use Training in three ways:

- **Overview** shows assigned training by agent
- **Configure** is where you build, edit, assign, duplicate, and archive training programs
- **My Training** shows training assigned to you

Agents usually only need **My Training**, where active assignments open the training player and archived assignments are kept behind **Show archived**.

## Build a Training Program

Use **Configure** when you need a repeatable training program.

1. Click **Create Training**
2. Enter the training name and description
3. Add modules in the order agents should complete them
4. Save as **Draft** while you are still building
5. Set the program to **Active** when it is ready to assign

Active programs can be assigned to agents. Drafts let you keep working without exposing unfinished training.

## Pick Module Types

Choose modules based on what you want agents to practice:

- Use **Learn** for manager-authored training content
- Use **Review** when agents should evaluate a real call against rubric prompts
- Use **Simulate** when agents should practice a scenario with an AI caller
- Use **Quiz** when you need multiple-choice or multi-select knowledge checks

Training progress is saved as agents complete modules. Review, simulate, and quiz modules can also keep attempt history for replay and manager inspection.

## Monitor Completion

Use **Overview** to check who has training assigned, which programs are in progress, and which modules have been completed.

Expand an agent when you need dates, status, or module-level progress before following up.
