The Daily Report page shows an AI-generated summary of the previous 24 hours of QA activity.

## Report Sections

### 1. Recommendations

Two categories of AI-generated recommendations:

- **Team-wide** - Trends affecting multiple agents or the whole team, with severity levels (High, Medium, Low)
- **Individual** - Specific action items for individual agents, prioritized by impact

Click the arrow icon next to any recommendation to jump to the relevant Coaching or Training page.

### 2. At a Glance

Quick metrics for the day:

- **Average QA Score** - Team average with trend description
- **Total Calls Analyzed** - Number of calls scored
- **Red Flags** - Count of calls with red flags

### 3. Standout Agents

Agents with notably high performance, showing their average score and call count.

### 4. Coaching Opportunities

Agents and specific metrics where they're struggling. Shows failure count and miss rate for each metric.

For agent users, this section appears as "Improvement Areas" and shows only their own data.

### 5. Red Flags

Summary of red flag alerts from the day, including alert type, description, severity, and affected count.

## Report Generation

Reports are generated automatically and show data from the previous 24 hours. The report creation timestamp appears at the top of the page.
